Country records were selected from `data/country-codes.csv` at the source above on 2026-09-20. The exact source CSV is not needed at runtime. `data/countries.json` is the complete human-reviewable curated subset. Public-domain source data remains public domain. The selection contains 80 sovereign countries. Common names for the US and UK are expanded, Washington is displayed as Washington, D.C., and the outdated Nur-Sultan entry is corrected to Astana (see the [Kazakhstan government](https://www.gov.kz/article/19305?lang=en)). Switzerland and Malaysia are excluded from capital questions to avoid federal-city or administrative-capital ambiguity. Shapes with remote territories or difficult archipelagos are excluded individually.

Strategoi was inspected at `/home/pedro/Strategoi/packages/shared/src/data/globe-geometry.ts`. It uses world-atlas, topojson-client, and world-countries. GeoTandem reuses the open geographic dependency approach, not Strategoi source code. GeoTandem uses the public-domain country-codes dataset instead of the ODbL-licensed world-countries dataset.

Other application dependencies include React (MIT), Express (MIT), Socket.IO and socket.io-client (MIT), Zod (MIT), Helmet (MIT), and Lucide (ISC; some incorporated icons have MIT notices). Their upstream notices ship in the installed npm packages. Exact installed versions are pinned by `package-lock.json`.

Borders are generalized cartographic data for a recreational quiz and do not express a political position. Curated eligibility avoids dependencies and countries with particularly ambiguous naming or capital conventions. Generated silhouettes are isolated, fitted into a consistent canvas, and contain no labels or adjacent countries.

Silhouette normalization centers the projection on each country to avoid a date-line seam through New Zealand. Minor islands below 0.5% of the largest polygon are omitted for legibility. Portugal, Spain, and Ecuador use their continental mainland silhouette so distant island groups do not shrink the recognizable outline. This display choice affects only shape questions.
